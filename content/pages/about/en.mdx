## Who we are

NetworkAI Technology Co., Ltd is a consultancy, design and system integration company based in Da Nang, specializing in ICT, ELV, Smart Building and Hospitality Technology solutions.

With over 18 years of industry experience, our team has delivered consultancy, design, design review and technical supervision services for hospitality projects operated by leading international brands, including Hilton, Hyatt, Marriott, Accor, IHG, Wyndham, Radisson and Aman Resorts.

## What we do

Through these projects we have developed extensive expertise in international hospitality standards, operational requirements and technology planning for luxury hotels, resorts, mixed-use developments and high-end residences.

Beyond consulting and design, we provide end-to-end project delivery services, from system integration and commissioning to operational support, ensuring reliable, scalable and future-ready technology solutions.
